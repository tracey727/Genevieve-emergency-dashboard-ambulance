# GENEVIEVE Emergency Health, Ambulance & Patient Transport™ — Command and Production Module V2.7

A separate emergency-response, transfer and patient-transport sector foundation built on GENEVIEVE HEALTH™ Production Foundation V2, the GENEVIEVE Accountability Core and the mandatory Queensland emergency framework.

## Status

**Fictional or coded data only.** This package is not a Triple Zero, computer-aided dispatch, ambulance, retrieval, hospital, clinical-record or patient-transport authority. It does not replace QAS systems, current clinical protocols, registered clinicians, emergency services or statutory duties. Production mode fails closed until every technical and governance gate is supplied.

## Main capabilities

- emergency call intake, location, access, hazard and dispatch ownership
- scene, crew, vehicle and interagency safety
- pre-hospital clinical observations, procedures, medicines and protocol evidence
- destination, pre-notification and hospital transfer-of-care accountability
- interfacility and time-critical transfer coordination
- Retrieval Services Queensland and aeromedical workflow support
- non-emergency patient transport, recurring trips, pickup and return continuity
- mental-health transport, supporter, property, pet and dependant arrangements
- fleet, oxygen, equipment, medicines, communications, infection and cleaning readiness
- workforce registration, credentialing, driver, fatigue, supervision and WHS
- Queensland facility codes, Australian Warning System and GENEVIEVE accountability separation
- PostgreSQL tenant isolation, append-only clinical and handover evidence, and blocked premature closure

## Mandatory boundaries

- Authorised dispatchers, clinicians, retrieval teams, hospitals, transport providers and emergency agencies retain every triage, treatment, medicine, destination, asset, flight and transfer decision.
- A unit notified, booking created, patient loaded, pre-notification sent or vehicle arriving is not automatically an outcome.
- No scene entry is directed by the software.
- No patient or transport is closed without accepted handover, outcome and evidence.
- GENEVIEVE LISTENS™ remains separate in code, data, repository, storage namespace and deployment.
